About

Built on site, not in a spreadsheet

DGMO Consultancy Limited is a principal-led practice — twenty years on megaprojects across roads, ports, airports, data centres and semiconductor.

Construction manager on the M11 and Gort–Tuam road schemes, on Dublin Port and Liverpool Port, and on the Meta campus at Clonee. Logistics on the Liverpool Airport works. Then site logistics with Arcadis across Malaysia, Germany and the United States, and with DPS Engineering on one of Europe's largest semiconductor programmes, where the practice runs live site services today and a standing capacity model against them.

The methods here were built on those sites, not adapted from a transport assessment: demand ratios from observed usage, never allocation lists, every figure carrying its source and verification date.
