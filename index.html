About

Built on site, not in a spreadsheet

DGMO Consultancy Limited is a principal-led practice — twenty years on megaprojects across roads, ports, airports, data centres and semiconductor, in Ireland, the United Kingdom, Germany, Malaysia and the United States.

The principal's career ran through construction management on national road schemes, port works and a hyperscale data-centre campus, then site logistics for international engineering consultancies on industrial programmes across three continents — including the day-to-day running of parking, bussing, welfare and access at megaproject scale, and a standing capacity model held against them.

The methods here were built on those sites, not adapted from a transport assessment: demand ratios from observed usage, never allocation lists, every figure carrying its source and verification date.

Terms

Every engagement is a fixed fee, quoted in writing before any work begins. Capacity-reach dates and decide-by dates are forecasts, issued at a stated confidence, and are not a warranty of outcome. Client data is held in confidence under the client's own NDA and is never reused, quoted or carried across to another engagement. Everything shown publicly on this site, in the film and in the demonstration pack is synthetic. Ratios are inputs carrying a source and a confidence marker, and the client may substitute their own at any time. Nothing here is planning, legal or engineering advice; where a remedy needs consent, that is flagged as "planning advice required". Contracts are governed by the law of Ireland.
